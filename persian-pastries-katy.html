Questions

How is baklava sold?

Baklava is $35 per tray. Most cookies are sold by the pound.

Yes. Message us early with the quantities and date so we can bake everything fresh for you.

Are napoleon and piroshki sold by the piece?

Yes — napoleon is $6 and piroshki $5 each, with a minimum order of 5.
