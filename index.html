Our Story

Two kitchens, one table

Elly's Bakery started as a home kitchen in Katy filling orders one cake at a time. Today we bake celebration cakes, cookies, and rolled pastries alongside a full menu of traditional Persian dishes — from saffron rice to slow-simmered khoresht — so you can order dessert and dinner from the same place.

Made to order — every cake, tray, and pot is baked or cooked after you order, not sitting on a shelf.
Two menus, one kitchen — Western-style bakery favorites and traditional Persian home cooking.

Bakery in Katy, TX

Cakes, Persian pastries & Persian food in Katy

Looking for a bakery near you in Katy, Texas? Elly's Bakery is a small home bakery making custom birthday cakes, Swiss rolls, baklava and Persian cookies, plus traditional Persian food for family dinners and parties. Everything is made fresh to order for pickup in Katy — an easy drive from Cinco Ranch, Fulshear, Richmond, Brookshire and west Houston.

How do I order a custom birthday cake in Katy?

Text a photo or idea of the cake you want to (832) 799-8204, or DM @ellysbakery_tx on Instagram, with your date and number of guests. We confirm the design and price, bake it fresh, and you pick it up in Katy. Medium birthday cakes start at $50.

Where can I buy baklava and Persian sweets near Katy?

Right here. We bake baklava by the tray ($35) and Persian cookies by the pound: nan-e berenji (rice cookies), nan-e nokhodchi (chickpea cookies), latifeh, Tabriz ghorabiyeh, coconut cookies and shirini khamei (Persian cream puffs). Popular for Nowruz, Yalda, Eid, weddings and tea with guests.

Do you cater Persian food for parties?

Yes. We cook traditional Persian dishes for gatherings: ghormeh sabzi, fesenjan, gheymeh, zereshk polo, baghali polo ba mahicheh, kofteh Tabrizi, ash reshteh, kashk-e bademjan, mirza ghasemi, dolmeh and more. Tell us how many guests and we'll help you plan the trays.

Do you deliver?

Not at this time. All orders are picked up in Katy, TX, and we send the pickup details when we confirm your order.

How far ahead should I order?

The earlier the better, especially for custom cakes, large catering orders, weekends and holidays. Text us your date and we'll tell you right away if it's available.
